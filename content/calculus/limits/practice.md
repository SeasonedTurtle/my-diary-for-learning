1. Pick f(x) = 2x and a number a. Compute f at a - 0.1 and at a + 0.1. See which number those outputs surround.
2. Repeat with a - 0.01 and a + 0.01. The outputs should sit even closer to the same number.
3. For (x^2 - 1) / (x - 1), do not plug in x = 1. Factor the top, cancel the shared (x - 1), and evaluate x + 1 at 1. That value is the limit, not a function value.
4. If the two sides do not agree, write that the limit does not exist.
