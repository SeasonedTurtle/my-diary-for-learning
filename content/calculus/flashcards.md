# Card
Front: What two questions does calculus study?
Back: How fast something is changing, and how much something adds up.

# Card
Front: What does a derivative measure?
Back: How fast a quantity is changing at a moment.

# Card
Front: What does an integral measure?
Back: How much a quantity has accumulated.

# Card
Front: What idea sits underneath both derivatives and integrals?
