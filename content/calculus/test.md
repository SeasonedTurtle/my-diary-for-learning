# Question
Prompt: Calculus is mainly about which pair of ideas?
Choices:
- Change and accumulation
- Shapes and angles
- Data and averages
Answer: Change and accumulation
Why: Derivatives study change. Integrals study accumulation.

# Question
Prompt: What does a derivative tell you?
Choices:
- How fast something is changing
- The total amount accumulated
- The average of a list
Answer: How fast something is changing
Why: A derivative is an instantaneous rate of change.

# Question
Prompt: Name the idea that both derivatives and integrals are built on.
Answer: a limit | limit | limits | the limit
Why: A limit describes the value a process approaches. Derivatives and integrals are defined from limits.
